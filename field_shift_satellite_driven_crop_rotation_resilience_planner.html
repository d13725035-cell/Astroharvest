Data Architecture & Scoring Engine

NASA Earth Observation Telemetry

How multi-sensor satellite observations power Field Shift's multi-criteria crop rotation model.

NASA SMAP (L4 Soil Moisture) 9 km / 3-hr

Measures surface (0–5 cm) and root-zone (0–100 cm) volumetric soil moisture to quantify historical drought onset and infiltration efficiency.

NASA GPM (IMERG Precip) 0.1° / 30-min

Captures rain intensity extremes to flag topsoil erosion risk during fallow periods and identify windows for cover crop seeding.

ISS ECOSTRESS (ESI / ET) 70 m Thermal

Monitors Evaporative Stress Index (ESI) at field scale, detecting plant water stress weeks before visible leaf wilting occurs.

NASA POWER & HLS NDVI 30 m / Daily

Combines agroclimatology (Growing Degree Days, solar radiation, heat extremes) with Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 biomass history.

Multi-Criteria Agronomic Scoring Algorithm

Every 4-year crop sequence is evaluated across four dynamic indices weighted by the farmer's live preference sliders ($W_w, W_s, W_p, W_b$) and adjusted for local soil texture retention factors and climate stress multipliers:

ResilienceScore = Σ [ W_i × NormScore_i(CropTraits, NASA_Anomalies, SoilTexture) ] / Σ W_i
  • Botanical Family Rotation Penalty: Consecutive crops in the same botanical family (e.g., Poaceae or Solanaceae) trigger a pest/pathogen carryover penalty.
  • Root Architecture Synergy: Alternating deep taproots (radish, sunflower, pigeon pea) with fibrous grass roots breaks hardpan and increases SMAP root-zone infiltration.
  • Nitrogen & Carbon Mass Balance: Legume nitrogen fixation credits reduce synthetic N requirements for subsequent high-demand cereal crops.
